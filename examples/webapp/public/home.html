HOW IT WORKS

01 · HASH

Fingerprint locally

Your text or file is hashed with SHA-256 on your side. The document itself is never uploaded.

02 · BATCH

Merkle tree

All hashes from the last hour become leaves of one Merkle tree. Thousands of hashes, one root.

03 · ANCHOR

One OP_RETURN

Only the 32-byte root goes into a single Bitcoin transaction. The server pays the fee.

04 · PROVE

Verify yourself

Your proof re-builds the root and checks it against the real transaction and block. No trust in btcnotar needed.
